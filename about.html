Engineering Excellence

Neon Flip was built to push the limits of mobile and desktop browser performance. Operating at a silky smooth 60 FPS, it renders glowing neon particle fields, laser gates, and custom ball skins with zero external dependencies.

Procedural Audio

All music and sound effects are synthesized on the fly via the Web Audio API, generating driving 128 BPM retro synthwave arpeggios and punchy basslines without requiring megabytes of audio downloads.

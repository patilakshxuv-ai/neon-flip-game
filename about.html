About Neon Flip

One tap to invert gravity. Hypersonic arcade reflexes.

How the Game Works

Neon Flip is a browser-based arcade game rendered with HTML Canvas. Tap the screen or press Space to switch gravity, avoid obstacles, and collect coins. Your score and unlocked items are saved locally in your browser. Frame rate depends on your device and browser.

Audio and Customization

The game creates its music and sound effects with the Web Audio API. Players can unlock different ball skins using coins collected during runs.

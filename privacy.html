Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Welcome to Neon Flip: One Tap Challenge. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent information practices. This policy details how we treat player data, cookies, and our partnership with Google AdSense.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Neon Flip is designed as a client-side instant arcade game. We do not require registration, passwords, credit cards, or telephone numbers. No personal profiling data is stored on remote private servers.

2. Local Storage & Offline Persistence

To preserve your game progress without requiring an account, the game stores data locally on your device via localStorage:

  • neon_flip_save_v1: Stores coins, unlocked ball skins, high scores, audio volume levels, and daily streak progress.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the game page. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • To opt out of third-party cookie usage generally, visit www.aboutads.info.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Cookie preferences can be managed directly via our on-site cookie controls.

5. GDPR Compliance (EEA & UK)

On the game page, the consent banner lets you accept or decline ad requests. If you decline, the page does not load its AdSense script. Depending on your location, applicable Google consent requirements may require additional controls.
